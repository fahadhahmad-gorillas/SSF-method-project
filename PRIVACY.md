# Privacy Policy — Gorillas: Banana Battle

_Last updated: 2026-06-06_

**Gorillas: Banana Battle** ("the app") is a single-player and local two-player
arcade game. We respect your privacy.

## What we collect
**Nothing.** The app does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal
information. It has no user accounts, no login, and no analytics or tracking.

## Data storage
The app may store game settings and progress **locally on your device only**
(in the browser/app cache). This data never leaves your device and is not
accessible to us or any third party.

## Network access
The app works fully offline. It does not send data to any server.

## Third parties / ads
The app contains **no advertising** and **no third-party SDKs**.

## Children
The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect any data from
anyone, including children under 13.
